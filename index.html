The Problem

Retail investors lack institutional-grade tools for portfolio optimization, risk control, and probability-based goal estimation.

Historical Returns Bias

Over-reliance on historical returns without forward-looking risk metrics creates vulnerability during market regime shifts.

Hidden Risk Blindspots

Ignoring volatility, correlation, and tail-risk in portfolio construction leads to severe unhedged losses.

Absence of Stress-Testing

No structured drawdown analysis or stress-testing capabilities leaves capital exposed to severe historical drawdowns.

Deterministic Planning

No probabilistic financial planning or goal-based simulation tools to calculate realistic likelihoods of reaching wealth targets.
